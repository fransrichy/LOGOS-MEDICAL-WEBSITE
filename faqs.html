Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about appointments, services, and billing.

You can book an appointment either by calling our reception desk at **+264 61 XXX XXX** during operating hours, or by filling out the secure inquiry form on our Contact Page. We recommend booking in advance.

Logos Medical Services accepts most major Namibian medical aid schemes. Please call our billing department to confirm your specific scheme's coverage. We also accept cash, card (Visa/Mastercard), and EFT payments.

While our general practitioners are equipped for primary care, most specialists require a referral from a GP, especially for medical aid purposes. We can arrange a consultation with one of our GPs to facilitate a timely referral.
