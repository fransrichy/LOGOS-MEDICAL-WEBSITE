Our Vision

To be the {most trusted and preferred primary healthcare provider in Namibia}, recognized for excellence, innovation, and compassionate care.

Our Mission

To deliver comprehensive, affordable, and high-quality medical services using a patient-centered approach, promoting health and wellness for all members of the community.

Our History & Commitment

Logos Medical Services was founded in 2024 by Logos Medical Team with the vision of filling a critical need for accessible, professional medical care in the rapidly growing Pioneers Park area of Windhoek.

Starting as a small family practice, the center quickly grew, building a reputation for meticulous care and patient trust. Over the years, we have expanded our services to include specialist referrals, advanced testing, and a dedicated women's health clinic, always adhering to the highest standards of medical ethics and practice.

Today, we stand as a beacon of health in Namibia, continually investing in our team and technology to ensure the best possible outcomes for our patients.
